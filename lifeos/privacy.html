LifeOS Privacy Policy

LifeOS is a private personal assistant used only by its owner, Ben Elkayam.

  • Access: LifeOS has read-only access to the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar.
  • Use: This data is used only to build the owner's daily brief.
  • Storage: Data is stored in the owner's private database.
  • Sharing: Data is never shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Revoking access: Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
